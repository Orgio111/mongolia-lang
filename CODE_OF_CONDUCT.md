# Community conduct

Treat participants respectfully across languages, experience levels and backgrounds. Critique
technical decisions with evidence. Harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory abuse and unwanted
sexual attention are unacceptable. Do not disclose private information or retaliation against
reporters. Maintainers may remove harmful content, warn, restrict or remove participation, using
proportionate measures and recording reasons privately. Before opening public community spaces,
designate a confidential contact and an appeal reviewer independent of the original decision.
No contact address or enforcement organization is assumed to exist yet.
